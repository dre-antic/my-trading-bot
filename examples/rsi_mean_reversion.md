# RSI Mean Reversion

This is how I trade.

I only trade Bitcoin (BTC/USDT) on the 1 hour chart.

## Entry

Buy when the 14-period RSI drops below 30 and price is above the 200 EMA.

## Exit

Sell when RSI goes above 70.
Stop loss 2%.
Take profit 4%.

## Risk

$10 USDT per trade.
One position at a time.
