# Do Lottery Winners Really Go Broke?

**Title:** Do Lottery Winners Really Go Broke? (The 70% Myth)

Alternative titles:
- The Lottery "Fact" Nobody Can Source
- What Actually Happens When You Win the Lottery
- Why Winning the Lottery Doesn't Fix Money Problems

**Thumbnail idea:** a shocked mannequin character next to a huge "70%?" with the question mark in red, on the cream background.

**Description:**
You've heard that 70% of lottery winners go broke. Nobody can find the study. Here's what the real research on lottery winners in Florida and Sweden shows, and why money acts as an amplifier for your habits.

Sources:
- National Endowment for Financial Education, statement that the "70% of lottery winners go broke" figure is not from its research (2018)
- Hankins, Hoekstra & Skiba (2011), "The Ticket to Easy Street? The Financial Consequences of Winning the Lottery", Review of Economics and Statistics
- Lindqvist, Östling & Cesarini (2020), "Long-Run Effects of Lottery Wealth on Psychological Well-Being", Review of Economic Studies
- Powerball jackpot odds: 1 in 292,201,338 (powerball.com)
