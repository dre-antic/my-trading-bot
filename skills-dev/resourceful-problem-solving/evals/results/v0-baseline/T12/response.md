## What to set up today

Do these in order. Each step is free to start. A card processor takes a percentage only when someone pays you, and it will ask for your legal name, an ID, and a bank account before the first payout.

1. **Make a Google account** if you don’t have one, on the Chromebook.
2. **Put the workouts on YouTube.** Use [YouTube Studio](https://studio.youtube.com). Upload each video as **Unlisted**. Unlisted videos don’t appear in search, and anyone with the link can still open them, so send the link only after someone has paid. Title them clearly: `Week 1 · Day 1 · Full body · 25 min`. Add the equipment and the level in the first line of the description.
3. **Build one client page on Google Sites.** [sites.google.com](https://sites.google.com) → Blank site → publish to the free `sites.google.com/...` address. On a phone this is the “app”: they save it to the home screen. Put only public information on the page.
4. **Turn on payments.** Create [Stripe Payment Links](https://dashboard.stripe.com/payment-links) or a PayPal payment link for each offer (4-week start, 8-week coaching, a single check-in). Paste those buttons on the page. Leave prices and what they get in plain language next to each button.
5. **Use a chat app clients already have.** WhatsApp, iMessage, or Telegram. Put one “Message me” link on the page. Keep client progress, injuries, and photos in that private thread, never on the public site.
6. **Add a weekly check-in form.** Google Forms → send the responses to a Google Sheet. Ask: name, workouts finished, energy from 1 to 5, anything that hurt, one win, one question.

### Page outline you can paste

**Headline:** Coaching that fits a normal week.

**Short intro:** Who you train, the result you help with, and how many days a week it takes. One or two sentences. Stay in coaching. Leave diagnosis and treatment to a clinician.

**How it works**

- You pay for the block.
- I send that week’s unlisted workouts.
- You train, then send the check-in.
- I reply with the next week’s plan.

**Offers**

- 4-week start — price, what’s included, payment button
- Ongoing coaching — price per 4 weeks, what’s included, payment button

**This week’s sample:** embed one public YouTube video so a stranger can see how you coach. Keep the paid library unlisted.

**Book / message:** link to your chat app, and your check-in form.

### Message to send after someone pays

> You’re in. Here is this week’s playlist: [unlisted link]. Train the days in order. When you finish the week, send this check-in: [form link]. If something hurts, stop that exercise and message me before the next session. Reply here with the time of day you can train, and I’ll match the week to that.

### A week you can run without an app

- **Sunday:** upload or refresh 3 unlisted workouts and update the site’s sample if you want.
- **Monday:** send the playlist link only to people who have paid for this block.
- **Wednesday:** answer check-ins. Ten focused minutes per client is enough at the start.
- **Friday:** confirm who is renewing and send the payment link for the next block.

That is a real coaching service. Clients on iPhone and Android both open the same links. Nothing is waiting on Apple or Google.

## When an app is actually the next step

An installable app starts to earn its cost when the manual version is slowing you down: a roster you can’t track in a sheet, people sharing video links, or clients asking for a home-screen app often enough that it costs you sales.

Budget the real minimum before any build:

- $99 for Apple, every year, plus $25 once for Google.
- A Mac, a cloud Mac, or a developer, because the iPhone build can’t be made on the Chromebook.
- A video host and a chat/backend plan once you outgrow YouTube unlisted links and WhatsApp.
- About three extra weeks on Google Play for a new personal account, because of the 12-tester, 14-day closed test, then review.
- Store commission on digital goods sold inside the app. Coaching you deliver and charge for on the web can stay on Stripe or PayPal.

A solid first version is a logged-in library, a payment record, and a message thread. Live “both stores by Friday” is a later project with a named budget, your ID, and a build machine.
