You can have clients training, watching your videos, paying you, and messaging you from their phones today. An app under your own name cannot be live on the App Store and Google Play by Friday.

Those goals conflict. Apple charges **$99 per year** to publish on the App Store. Its fee waiver is only for nonprofits, accredited schools, and government bodies, and it excludes individuals, sole proprietors, and anyone selling digital services. A fitness coaching business does not qualify. Google charges a **$25** registration fee, and a new personal Play account created after November 13, 2023 must keep **12 testers opted in for 14 days straight** before it can even apply to go public. Review after that can take from a few hours up to **7 days**, and Google’s own guidance is to leave at least a week. Today is Friday, November 20, 2026, so the 14-day test alone runs past next Friday. A Chromebook also cannot run Apple’s developer tools, and a custom app would need someone technical to keep it updated.

Use the coaching app that is already in both stores, and stay on the free plan.

## What to use

**Everfit Starter, plus YouTube, plus a Stripe payment link.**

Everfit’s client app is already on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/everfit-train-smart/id1438926364) (“Everfit - Train smart”) and on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.everfit). Clients install that app. You coach from the Everfit website in Chrome on the Chromebook. No install, no code, and no card to start. The free plan covers 5 clients, a workout builder, the built-in exercise library (the pricing page says 1,000+ exercises), progress tracking, and inbox chat where you can send images and videos. Clients do not pay Everfit.

Money stays outside Everfit. Their payments add-on is **$9 a month**, so leave it off. A [Stripe Payment Link](https://docs.stripe.com/payment-links) has no monthly fee. On the US pricing page, a successful domestic card payment costs **2.9% + 30¢**. On a $100 payment that is $3.20, and $96.80 is what settles to your bank before any tax. Fees in other countries differ. Stripe only takes that cut when a client actually pays, so you do not need cash up front.

This is the setup working coaches use at the start. The icon on the phone says Everfit, and your name shows as the coach. Your own logo and colors are a paid feature. That trade is what makes this possible at $0, on a Chromebook, with no programming.

If Everfit will not let you sign up in your country, or you already have more than 5 clients, use the backup at the end. Same videos, same payment link, email for chat.

## What I checked

- Apple membership is $99 per year, listed on the [Apple Developer Program](https://developer.apple.com/programs/) page. The [fee-waiver rules](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/fee-waivers/) exclude a for-profit one-person coaching business. Apple says it reviews at least half of submissions in under 24 hours and 90% in under 48 hours, and that apps with little review history can take longer ([App Review](https://developer.apple.com/distribute/app-review/)). Enrollment still requires identity verification and the $99, which you do not have.
- Google’s [signup steps](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6112435) list a one-time $25 fee. [Testing rules](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465) require the 12-tester, 14-day closed test for new personal accounts before production access. [Publishing help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859654) says to plan at least a week because review can take up to 7 days or longer.
- Everfit’s [pricing page](https://everfit.io/pricing/), as fetched November 20, 2026: Starter is free for 5 clients and includes the client apps, workout builder, and inbox. Pro starts at $19 a month. On-demand “Netflix-style” collections are a $25 a month add-on ($21 billed annually) or part of the much larger Studio plan. Payments are a separate $9 a month add-on. The FAQ says clients start by signing up with email.
- Everfit’s help center says **uploading a video file is paid-only**, while a **YouTube or Vimeo link** can be attached to an exercise. Private YouTube videos will not play. Unlisted or public will ([demo videos](https://help.everfit.io/en/articles/4701854-can-i-add-my-own-demo-video)).
- Stripe’s [pricing page](https://stripe.com/pricing), checked November 20, 2026: no setup fee and no monthly fee, 2.9% + 30¢ per successful US domestic card charge, and Payment Links included at no extra charge. I did not confirm a separate fee for subscriptions, so use a one-time link for a block of coaching rather than a recurring membership.
- Hevy Coach is a 30-day trial and then $25 a month ([Hevy Coach pricing](https://hevycoach.com/pricing/)). That fails a zero budget, so it is out.

## Watch out for

- **Five clients is the wall.** The free plan stops at 5. Everfit’s monthly Pro prices are published at 5 clients for $19 and 10 clients for $29. There is no free seat for client number 6. Keep a waiting list until one payment can cover the upgrade, or switch to the backup.
- **The app is Everfit’s, and it is not perfect.** The App Store listing showed 4.7 from about 2,700 ratings. Google Play showed 3.8 from about 1,400 reviews, last updated November 4, 2026. Send yourself through the client app once before you promise a particular screen to a client.
- **Stripe verification is the slow part.** You need your legal name and a bank account. Start that signup today. Invite the first client into the app even if the payment link is not ready yet. Do not ask anyone to type a card number into chat.
- **Everfit can change the free plan.** Keep a simple Google Sheet with client name, email, start date, and amount paid. Leave health details in Everfit, not in the sheet. Your YouTube channel and Stripe account stay yours if you ever leave.
- **Coaching is not medical care.** Use the clearance line in the welcome message below. Emergencies go to local emergency services, not the in-app chat.
- I did not look up business-license or sales-tax rules for your area. Check those before you charge. Leave Stripe Tax off until you know you need it.

## Do this today, in Chrome

1. Open [everfit.io/pricing](https://everfit.io/pricing/) and start **Starter**. Use your email. Stop if it asks for a credit card. That would mean you are not on the free plan.
2. If you have a phone, use the onboarding checklist item **Invite Myself**, install the client app from the store link in that email, and look at what a client sees. If you only have the Chromebook, skip this and keep going.
3. Build one workout from exercises already in their library. Search for the moves in the first-week plan below. Assign that workout on the client’s training calendar. Skip any “On-demand” or “Video storage” screen that asks you to pay.
4. For a follow-along video, film it on a phone if you have one, otherwise the Chromebook camera is enough for a short demo. Upload it to YouTube, set visibility to **Unlisted**, and paste the link into the exercise. Private will not play inside Everfit. If the file-upload button asks you to upgrade, leave it. The link is the free method.
5. Add your first client: on the homepage, **Add Client** (top right) → **Add Single Client**. Enter first name, last name, email, and type **Online**, then **Invite**. They get an email, create a profile, and install Everfit on their phone. You can also copy the invite link and text it.
6. Open that client, go to **Inbox**, and send the welcome message below. Put their answers in the client’s **Notes**.
7. Create a free [Stripe](https://stripe.com) account in your own legal name and connect your bank. Then create one **Payment Link** for a single 4-week package (Payment Links in the Stripe dashboard). Paste that link into the same chat only after Stripe has approved the account. Leave Everfit’s Payments add-on turned off.

Reply in the Everfit inbox once a day, at a time you actually keep. That is enough for a professional coaching practice at this size.

## First week you can assign now

Equipment: a chair and a pair of water bottles, or nothing. After they answer the intake questions, swap in gym moves if they have a gym.

**Day 1, strength.** Search the library for each line and pick the version with a demo video.

- Sit to stand from a chair, 3 sets of 8
- Wall or counter push-up, 3 sets of 8
- Hip hinge (they bow at the hips with a flat back), 3 sets of 8
- Row with a backpack or water bottles, 3 sets of 8
- Plank on a counter or the floor, 3 holds of 20 seconds

**Day 2, easy walk.** 20 minutes at a pace where they can talk in full sentences.

**Day 3, strength.**

- Step back into a lunge, holding a chair, 3 sets of 6 each leg
- Glute bridge on the floor, 3 sets of 10
- Overhead press with water bottles, 3 sets of 8
- Bird dog, 3 sets of 6 each side

Walk on two other days if they feel good. Rest the remaining days. Tell them to stop any move that causes sharp pain.

## Your name on the stores, later

That step stays parked until coaching income can pay for it. It still needs the $99 Apple membership, the $25 Google fee, a finished app, 12 people willing to test for 14 days, and then store review. Everfit only offers an app under your own name on its Enterprise plan, and that price is not published. You would still have to pay Apple and Google yourself. Nothing about a branded listing makes the coaching better for the first five clients.

## If Everfit is blocked, or you pass 5 clients

Use four free tools you can run in the same Chrome browser. This path has no client cap. It does not put your own app in the stores. Clients use their phone browser and can add an icon to the home screen.

1. Create a free site at [sites.google.com](https://sites.google.com). One page: who you help, your YouTube playlist, and the Stripe payment link.
2. Put follow-along videos in an **Unlisted** YouTube playlist and insert that playlist on the site.
3. Use the same Stripe one-time payment link.
4. Do client chat in Gmail. Check it once a day. Use the same welcome message.
5. On the client’s phone: open the site in the browser, then Share → **Add to Home Screen** on iPhone, or the browser menu → **Add to Home screen** on Android.
